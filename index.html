Exploration Milestones

1957

Sputnik 1

The first artificial satellite begins the space age.

1969

Apollo 11

Humans land on the Moon and return safely to Earth.

1977

Voyager

Twin probes tour the outer planets and keep traveling beyond the heliosphere.

1990

Hubble

A space telescope changes how humanity sees galaxies, nebulae, and cosmic time.

2021

James Webb

Infrared observations reveal early galaxies, exoplanet chemistry, and stellar nurseries.

2024+

Artemis

NASA's lunar exploration program prepares crewed missions around and back to the Moon.
